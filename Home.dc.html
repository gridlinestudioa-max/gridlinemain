About the studio

Meet the studio
Who

A studio of one, rooted in small towns.

Gridline Studio is an independent practice, raised in small-town communities and now based in Chicago. It's built for the shops, cafés, and gyms that anchor a main street but rarely get design attention priced for them.

What

Brand work, priced à la carte.

Logos and typography, color systems, signage, social media, and websites, priced piece by piece, never hourly work, with a branding guide you can run on your own once we're done.

Why

So local brands look like themselves.

The goal isn't to turn every business into a corporate brand. It's to build an identity that reflects what makes it unique and helps it stand out in its own community.
